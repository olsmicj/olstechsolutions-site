Privacy Policy — Cheese Empire

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Developer: Olstech Studios ("we", "us")
Contact: michael@olstechsolutions.com

Cheese Empire is an offline, single-player game. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the game does and does not handle.

What the game itself collects: nothing leaves your device

  • No accounts. Cheese Empire has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.
  • No developer servers. We operate no backend for this game. Nothing you do in the game is transmitted to us.
  • Game progress stays on your device. Your save file (currency balances, generators, settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it. We cannot recover it, restore it, or see it.
  • No analytics of our own. The game contains no first-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Cheese Empire shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads appear only when you choose "Watch & Earn" inside the game. When ads are served, Google — not us — may collect and process data under Google's own policies, including:

  • Advertising ID (your device's resettable ad identifier) and other device identifiers
  • IP address, from which approximate (coarse) location may be derived
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, and tapped)

Google uses this data to serve, personalize (where you have allowed it), measure, and protect the ads system, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices). We do not receive your advertising ID or any of the data listed above; we see only aggregate, anonymized ad reports inside our AdMob account (for example, total ads shown per day).

Data sharing

We share no personal data with anyone, because we collect none. The only third party that processes data in connection with the game is Google (AdMob), as described above.

Children

Cheese Empire is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children — as stated above, we collect no data from anyone.

Security

Game data never leaves your device, and all ad traffic between the game and Google travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date before the change takes effect in a released version of the game.
