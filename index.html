VectorForge

Pictures in. Cut paths out.

Now in beta · v0.9.0-beta.30 · Free while we test

VectorForge turns pictures into clean, exact-size cut paths for laser cutters, CNC routers, and vinyl and paper cutting machines. Drop in an image, arrange it on a sheet of your material, fix the nodes right where they sit, and export a file your machine can cut.

It's a single 66 MB Windows app — no install, just run it. The beta is free; email or message us and the download link comes back to you.

Platform
Windows 10 / 11
Download

Files out
DXF · SVG, exact size
  • Traces as it lands. Drop in PNG, JPEG, WebP, HEIC, even camera RAW — it traces the moment the image arrives, with presets for line art, photos, and text.
  • Edit nodes in place. Open the nodes of every layer at once, right where they sit on the sheet — the fix other tracers make you export to do.
  • Your sheet is the material. Lay everything out on stock-sized sheets, with shapes and text alongside your artwork.
  • Exact at export. DXF (R12, one layer per colour) or SVG at real physical size in mm, cm, or inches — kerf compensation and holes-first cut order built in.

The part we obsessed over

Tracing is the easy half. The hard half is the cleanup — the stray point, the wobbly curve, the join that doesn't meet. VectorForge lets you grab nodes and handles on the sheet itself, across every layer at once, so the design you fix is the design you cut.
